Hi, I'm {{ site.first_name }} {{ site.last_name }}, a PhD student in the <a href="https://dtai.cs.kuleuven.be/" target="_blank">DTAI</a> research group at <a href="https://www.kuleuven.be/kuleuven/" target="_blank">KU Leuven</a>, supervised by <a href="https://wms.cs.kuleuven.be/people/lucderaedt" target="_blank">Luc De Raedt</a>. I work in the field of **neurosymbolic articial intelligence**, combining deep learning and symbolic reasoning. 

Learning is central to intelligence, and modern deep learning has proven highly effective in applications ranging from image recognition to language modeling and protein folding. Achieving state-of-the-art performance, however, requires ever-increasing amounts of data and compute. Integrating reasoning into deep learning models can dramatically improve data efficiency under the principle "do not learn what you already know". Yet current reasoning approaches do not scale, limiting their practical impact. Overcoming this scalability barrier is the challenge I am currently working on.
