Hi, I’m Rik Adriaensen, a PhD student in the DTAI research group at KU Leuven, supervised by Luc De Raedt. My research focuses on neurosymbolic artificial intelligence, combining deep learning and symbolic reasoning.

Learning is central to intelligence, and modern deep learning has proven highly effective in applications ranging from image recognition to language modeling and protein folding. However, achieving state-of-the-art performance requires ever-increasing amounts of data and computing power. Integrating reasoning into deep learning models can dramatically improve data efficiency based on the principle “do not learn what you already know”. Yet, current reasoning approaches do not scale, limiting their practical impact. Overcoming this scalability barrier is the challenge I am currently working on.
